# How to edit the DSP website

You don't need to know any code. All the words, photos, brothers and events live in a few files in the `content` folder, and there's a simple editor with normal text boxes and photo uploads for changing them.

## Logging in to the editor

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org** and click **Sign in with GitHub**.
2. Open the **dsp-website** repository. (Ask the VP of Branding & Media to add you to it if you don't see it.)
3. Down the left side you'll see: **Brothers, Recruitment, Home page, About page, Sponsorship, Contact & social links**.
4. Make your changes, then click **Save**. The live site updates on its own within a minute or two.

## Common jobs

### New semester: update the executive board
*Brothers → Executive board.* Change each person's name, role, photo and LinkedIn. Use the arrows to reorder people and the trash icon to remove someone.

Board members also stay listed in their own class. Update their role there too, so both places match.

### Add a new pledge class
*Brothers → Classes → Add an entry.* Give it a name (for example "Omega Class"), then add each brother. Classes show up on the site in the same order as the list, and every class gets its own filter button automatically.

### Add or change a brother
For each brother you can set:
- **Name**
- **Role.** Write `Brother` if they don't hold a position. Anything else shows up highlighted in purple.
- **Photo.** A portrait (taller than it is wide) works best. Faces should sit in the top third of the photo.
- **LinkedIn URL.** Paste the full link, like `https://www.linkedin.com/in/their-name/`. Leave it empty if they don't have one; their card then just won't link anywhere.

> Tip: open each LinkedIn link once after you save to make sure it goes to the right person.

### Update recruitment for next semester
*Recruitment.*
- Change the **page title** (for example "Spring 2027 recruitment").
- Edit the **events**. Pick the date from the calendar; the site fills in the day of the week by itself. Tick **Invite only** for interviews and other closed events.
- Swap in the new **flyer**.
- Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link into **Rush video link** once the video is out. Until then, the page shows "Coming soon".
- Add last semester's flyer to **Past flyers**.

### Change the president's letter
*Home page → President's letter.* Leave a blank line between paragraphs.

### Add a sponsor
*Sponsorship → Sponsors → Add an entry.* Upload their logo (PNG with a transparent background looks best) and, if you like, a link to their website.

## Photo tips
- Keep photos under 2–3 MB. Phone photos are fine; screenshots of photos are not.
- Use landscape photos for the gallery and pillars, and portraits for brothers.

## If something looks broken
Every change is saved in history, so nothing is ever lost. If a page stops loading after an edit, tell the VP of Branding & Media; they can undo the last change in one click on GitHub.
